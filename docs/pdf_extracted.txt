=== SLIDE 7 ===
RESEARCH OF SIMILAR SOLUTIONS
7@SIH Idea submission- Template
Existing Solution Advantages Disadvantages
PRAGATI (PMO/NIC, 
national project-monitoring 
platform)
Enables real-time monitoring of major 
government projects through PM-chaired reviews 
with video conferencing, geo-spatial mapping, and 
drone feeds; has monitored 3,300+ projects worth 
over ₹85 lakh crore with a 90%+ issue-resolution 
rate 
Built for large infrastructure/ministry-level 
projects reviewed monthly at the top, not 
designed for continuous, ground-level 
surveillance of individual institutes, NGOs, or 
field staff; no live CCTV , no random inspection 
assignment, no AI anomaly detection 
PM POSHAN Automated 
Monitoring System + state 
inspection apps (e.g. WB's 
MDM-Inspection app)
Uses IVRS/SMS/mobile apps to collect real-time 
data from schools with no internet dependency, 
and dedicated inspection apps exist specifically to 
identify irregularities at the school level
Relies heavily on self-reported data from the 
same institution being monitored, no 
independent video verification; inspections 
aren't AI-randomized, so visits remain 
predictable and can be anticipated by staff
SafetyCulture / iAuditor (now 
Mitti), global commercial 
inspection app
Lets teams digitize inspection forms, schedule 
recurring inspections, assign follow-up tasks, and 
log real-time issues, with photo and video evidence 
capture even offline; trusted by 70,000+ 
organizations
General-purpose commercial tool, not built for 
government welfare-scheme oversight; GPS 
tracking is still in early access and unreliable; 
subscription-based licensing, no live CCTV feed 
or surprise-VC capability, inspections scheduled 
by managers rather than randomized
